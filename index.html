How to read it: two firms split one market (sA + sB = 1). Growth rate depends on each firm's own share (network effect, curved by γ), pulled down by congestion (δ), and reduced if users can cheaply use both firms at once (low μ dampens the effect). The gauge shows whether the 50/50 point is a stable rest point (HOLDS) or a saddle that gets thrown to a corner (TIPS) — this is evaluated at the symmetric point regardless of your chosen starting share. Turn on "Firm A inflates its numbers" to watch growth get driven by a perceived share ŝ that drifts from the true one — and what happens when the gap gets caught.
